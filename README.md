# NEPSE Portfolio Risk Analysis

A Python-based analysis of risk, return, volatility, correlation, and diversification in Nepal’s stock market using historical NEPSE data.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes six companies listed on the Nepal Stock Exchange (NEPSE) from different sectors and evaluates their historical performance from 2022 to 2026.

The main objective is to examine whether diversification across different sectors can reduce portfolio risk and how a diversified portfolio performs compared with the NEPSE Index.

## Companies Analyzed

- Nabil Bank Limited (NABIL)
- Everest Bank Limited (EBL)
- Chilime Hydropower Company Limited (CHCL)
- Nepal Life Insurance Company Limited (NLIC)
- Shivam Cements Limited (SHIVM)
- Soaltee Hotel Limited (SHL)

## Analysis Performed

- Historical stock price analysis
- Daily return calculation
- Annualized volatility
- Correlation analysis
- Portfolio construction
- Diversification analysis
- Comparison with the NEPSE Index

## Tools Used

- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib

## Key Findings

- The diversified portfolio recorded annualized volatility of approximately 23.02%.
- Portfolio volatility was lower than five of the six individual stocks analyzed.
- The portfolio recorded a change of approximately -2.87%.
- The NEPSE Index increased by approximately 0.55% over the same period.
- The analysis shows that diversification reduced portfolio risk, although it did not outperform the market benchmark.
